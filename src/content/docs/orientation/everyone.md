This guide is for **anyone who serves on campus** — greeters, teachers, nursery, office, security, worship team. Read it once before your first service, then review annually.

## What you must know

| Know this | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| **Code Red** = evacuate | Leave the building; go to south parking lot |
| **Code Blue** = lockdown | Lock room, lights off, phones silent, stay put |
| **All clear** = only IC ends it | Do **not** press all clear yourself |
| **911** = life threat | Horns do **not** call the police |
| **Incident commander** | **Allan Miller** when on campus; elder on duty if he is out |

Weather shelter (only if IC directs): **lower auditorium** — not fellowship hall.

## Day-one checklist

- [ ] Read [Quick reference](/quick-reference/)
- [ ] Listen to [Campus audio](/system/campus-audio/) — Red, Blue, all clear, bells
- [ ] Open your room page under [Rooms](/rooms/) — know exit + assembly
- [ ] Check in on that room page with your **name** (no PIN needed for check-in)
- [ ] Add [alarm.arnoldcoc.org](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org) to your phone Home Screen (if you have a PIN)
- [ ] Know who is IC today and where the office is

## When you hear the horns

1. **Stop** what you are doing.
2. Match the tone: [Red](/codes/code-red/) leave · [Blue](/codes/code-blue/) lock down · Green = all clear (wait for IC word too).
3. Follow your **room lead** / area lead — do not invent a different plan.
4. Help visitors and children; stay calm.
5. After all clear: return only when IC says so; report anything that went wrong to IC or the office.

## What you never do

- Do **not** use phone PA (**ext 9090**) to declare Red or Blue.
- Do **not** open a locked door during Code Blue for someone you cannot verify.
- Do **not** post about an active incident on social media — see [Communication](/communication/).
- Do **not** call all clear unless you are IC or backup IC.
