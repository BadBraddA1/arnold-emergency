For **office / front desk** and anyone covering phones during services or office hours. Complete [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/) first.

## Your job in one line

Be the calm phone and visitor hub — **call 911** when needed, **never** declare codes on the PA, and point media to the spokesperson.

## Day-one checklist

- [ ] Finish [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/)
- [ ] Tour desk phone: how to dial out, how to reach IC / elders
- [ ] Know **ext 9090** is Alert Line (paging) — **not** for Red/Blue
- [ ] Read [Communication](/communication/) — media staging and “one voice”
- [ ] Know main entrance path for EMS / police
- [ ] Bookmark [Quick reference](/quick-reference/) and your desk [room page](/rooms/) if you have one (e.g. Secretaries Office)
- [ ] Check in on your room page with your Alert PIN

## When life is at risk

1. Call **911** (or confirm someone already did).
2. Send someone to the **main entrance** to meet EMS.
3. Notify IC / office lead as soon as practical.
4. Do **not** wait for horns — medical events usually stay local. See [Medical emergency](/medical-emergency/).

## During Code Red or Blue

| Do | Don't |
|----|--------|
| Call 911 if not already done | Use 9090 to “announce” evacuate or lockdown |
| Keep a simple log: time, who called, what you heard | Give interviews or post online |
| Direct reporters to spokesperson / staging | Let media into nursery or classrooms |
| Stay on phones for parents / staff as able | Open the building against an active Blue |

**Speaker tests:** desk phones may ring before test horns — press **0** to delay horns during **tests only**, never during a real code.

## After all clear

- Help IC with headcount questions from rooms.
- Resume normal phones; do not invent details for callers — point to official updates.
- If it was significant, expect [post-incident](/post-incident/) within 72 hours.
