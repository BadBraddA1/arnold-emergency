For **deacon over security**, ushers assigned to security, and anyone who carries an **Alert Fob** or Code Leader PIN. Complete [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/) first.

**Primary IC / deacon over security:** Allan Miller · **System Admin:** Adin Bradd

## Your job in one line

Keep the campus **armed and ready**, help people move correctly during a code, and support the incident commander — you do **not** freelance all clear.

## Day-one checklist

- [ ] Finish [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/)
- [ ] Confirm your Alert PIN scope (Code Leader / System Admin) with Adin if unsure — [Triggers](/triggers/)
- [ ] Add [Alert Mobile](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org) to Home Screen; open it once while on campus Wi‑Fi
- [ ] If you carry a fob: practice **Fob Arm** (3-hour window) before you need it — [Arnold Alert](/system/arnold-alert/)
- [ ] Walk main exits and know ADA exits (Level 1 east upper/lower; Level 2 west lower)
- [ ] Know assembly: **south parking lot**; weather only if IC directs → **lower auditorium**
- [ ] Read [Arm & standby](/system/arm-standby/) and [System limits](/system/limits/)

## Before services / events

1. Confirm Arnold Alert is **armed** (default). If it is on standby, ask why — IC or System Admin decides.
2. Fob carriers: arm fobs before you walk campus.
3. Know who is **IC** on site and who is covering office phones.
4. Spot-check that door cards / posters are still up in high-traffic rooms.

## During Code Red

1. Help clear hallways toward exits; assist mobility-impaired people to ADA exits.
2. Direct people to **south parking lot** — not cars first.
3. Report missing persons to IC.
4. Do not re-enter for belongings until IC authorizes.

## During Code Blue

1. Help lock or verify exterior / critical doors only if safe and IC directs.
2. Keep people **in place** — do not pull classrooms into hallways.
3. Meet law enforcement at the main entrance if IC assigns you.
4. Stay off personal social media.

## All clear

Only **IC** (or backup elder) ends the code. You may help verify zones are clear **after** IC asks.

| Task | Cadence | Notes |
