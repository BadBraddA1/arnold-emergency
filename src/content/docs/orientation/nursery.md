For **nursery** and **children’s ministry** workers. Complete [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/) and skim [Teaching](/orientation/teaching/) first — then this page.

Children move slower, panic differently, and reunification rules are stricter. Your calm voice is the plan.

## Your job in one line

Keep every child accounted for — evacuate or lock the suite with the roster, and release children **only** to authorized guardians.

## Day-one checklist

- [ ] Finish [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/)
- [ ] Open nursery / children’s room pages under [Rooms](/rooms/) (e.g. nursery, cry room) and check in with your name
- [ ] Walk the **nursery evacuation route** once with a lead
- [ ] Know where the **roster / check-in list** is every shift
- [ ] Know who may pick up each child (tag / list policy for your ministry)
- [ ] Know who notifies parents during an incident (usually nursery lead + IC timing)
- [ ] Read [Code Red](/codes/code-red/) and [Code Blue](/codes/code-blue/) nursery sections

## Code Red — Evacuate

1. Calm voice; take **roster** and any emergency bag your ministry keeps.
2. Follow the nursery / room exit plan — do not improvise through crowded stairs if your plan says otherwise.
3. Assemble at **south parking lot** (or IC-directed weather location: **lower auditorium**).
4. Count children against the roster. Report missing to IC **immediately**.
5. Reunify **only** with authorized guardians — no “I know them” exceptions under stress.

## Code Blue — Lockdown

1. Lock the nursery suite. Lights as your plan directs. Phones silent.
2. Keep children low, quiet, and occupied if possible — no hallway trips.
3. Do **not** open for unknown adults, even if they say they are a parent, until IC / police verify.
4. Stay put until all clear from IC.

## Parents and phones

- Parent notification is **coordinated with IC** — do not blast a group text that contradicts the active code.
- If parents arrive during Blue, keep them outside the suite until IC allows reunification.
- Office may field parent calls — keep your facts short and factual.

## Medical in nursery

Call **911** for life threat. Send someone to the main entrance for EMS. Usually **no** campus code unless IC orders one. See [Medical emergency](/medical-emergency/).
