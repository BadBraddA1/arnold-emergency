New to serving here? Start with **[Everyone](/orientation/everyone/)**, then read the guide for your role. Plan on **30–45 minutes** the first time — listen to the tones, find your room page, and know who the incident commander is.

| Guide | Who it's for |
|-------|----------------|
| [Everyone](/orientation/everyone/) | All volunteers, teachers, greeters, and staff with campus access |
| [Security](/orientation/security/) | Deacon over security, ushers with security duty, anyone carrying a fob |
| [Office](/orientation/office/) | Front desk, secretaries, anyone covering phones |
| [Teaching](/orientation/teaching/) | Classroom teachers, Bible class leads, adult class teachers |
| [Nursery & children](/orientation/nursery/) | Nursery and children's ministry workers |
| [Leadership](/orientation/leadership/) | Elders, IC backups, ministry leads who may take command |

## Before your first Sunday

1. Finish **Everyone** + your role guide.
2. Open **[Arnold Alert](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org)** and confirm your PIN works (ask System Admin if you don't have one).
3. Find **your room** under [Rooms](/rooms/) — review exit, then check in with your Alert PIN.
4. Listen to [Campus audio](/system/campus-audio/) once.
5. Keep [Quick reference](/quick-reference/) bookmarked.
