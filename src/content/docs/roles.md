## Incident commander (primary authority on site)

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | **Allan Miller** — always IC when on campus |
| **Backup** | Jason Powers, Kevin Isenberg, or Shannon Davidson (elder on duty) — only if Allan is unavailable |
| **May declare Code Red/Blue** | ☑ Yes |
| **May call All clear** | ☑ Yes — only after threat resolved |

**Responsibilities:**

- Decide Red vs. Blue (fire/smoke → usually Red; threat inside → usually Blue).
- Ensure **911** is called when required — the alarm does not dial 911.
- Assign someone to **account for children / nursery / classrooms** immediately.
- Authorize **all clear** — no one else without IC approval (backup assumes command if IC unavailable).

## Deacon over security

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | Allan Miller |
| **Backup** | Elder on duty |

**Responsibilities:**

- Know [Arnold Alert](/system/arnold-alert/) — app, fobs, Alert Desk.
- Confirm system is **armed** before services (default: armed).
- Help verify building zones before all clear.
- Run monthly **Alert Check** (speaker test) when building is empty.

## Nursery / children's ministry lead

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | Children's ministry lead on duty |
| **Backup** | Second adult in nursery suite |

**Responsibilities:**

- **Code Red:** evacuate per nursery plan; bring roster; reunify only with authorized guardians.
- **Code Blue:** lock nursery suite; silence; keep children calm; no opening for unknown persons.

## Worship / teaching lead (pulpit)

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | Worship or teaching lead on duty |

**Responsibilities:**

- On campus code: **stop service**; brief calm direction matching Red or Blue.
- Do **not** contradict the active code type.
- Defer to incident commander for all clear.

## Office / front desk

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | Office staff on duty |

**Responsibilities:**

- Monitor phones; **call 911** if not already done.
- **Do not** use ext 9090 (PA) for lockdown or evacuate.
- Desk phones may ring before speaker-test horns; press **0** to delay horns during **tests only**.
- **Direct media** to the spokesperson and staging area — see [Communication](/communication/).

## Spokesperson (media & social media)

| Field | Assignment |
|-------|------------|
| **Primary** | Designated spokesperson — confirm with office |
| **Backup** | IC or elder on duty |

**Responsibilities:**

- **Only voice** for press, official social accounts, and congregation-wide messages during/after an incident.
- Stay paired with IC for confirmed facts — no speculation.
- Follow the [media & social media playbook](/communication/#media-and-social-media-playbook).
- Pause scheduled social posts; protect privacy (especially children).

## All staff (general)

- **Arnold Alert** on phone Home Screen — [alarm.arnoldcoc.org](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org).
- Know your [Alert PIN](/system/arnold-alert/) scope.
- **Alert Fobs:** arm before carry ([Fob Arm](/system/arnold-alert/) — 3 hours).
- Report to your area lead; do not call all clear unless you are IC/backup.
