---
title: All clear
description: Who may end an active emergency code at Arnold Church of Christ.
---

**All clear** means the emergency is over and normal operations may resume.

## Who may call all clear

Only the **incident commander** or designated **backup** — not general staff or volunteers.

## How to trigger

1. IC confirms threat is resolved and areas are checked (coordinate with law enforcement when applicable).
2. Authorized person taps **Stop & All clear** in [Alert Mobile](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org) or **fob button 4** (when fob is armed).
3. Campus plays **Code Green** tone ×2.
4. Brief announcement: normal operations resume; counseling / debrief as needed.

## What all clear is not

- Not a separate “Code Green” drill button — it ends Red or Blue only.
- Not available when no code is active.
- Not a substitute for 911 standing down — follow dispatcher guidance.

## After all clear

See [Post-incident](/post-incident/) — debrief within 72 hours.
