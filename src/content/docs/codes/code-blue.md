---
title: Code Blue — Lockdown
description: Lockdown and shelter-in-place procedures for Arnold Church of Christ.
---

**When to use:** Threat inside the building, violent person, or incident commander orders shelter-in-place.

## Steps

1. **Trigger** Code Blue — [Alert Mobile](https://alarm.arnoldcoc.org), armed **Alert Fob** (button 2), or IC delegate.
2. **Call 911** if not already in progress.
3. **Everyone:** behind locked doors if possible; lights off; phones silent; away from windows and doors.
4. **Do not** gather in lobby or open areas. **Do not** evacuate unless IC or law enforcement directs.
5. **Nursery:** lock suite; adults between children and door.
6. **Pulpit / lobby:** stop movement; short calm instruction only.
7. **All clear** only when IC confirms with law enforcement / internal sweep as needed.

## Everyone

- **Locks** — lock door if you can; barricade only if trained to do so.
- **Lights off** — if safe and practical.
- **Silence** — phones on silent; no hallway traffic.
- **Stay** — remain until official all clear.

## Teachers & classroom leads

- Lock door; account for every student.
- Keep children quiet and away from windows.
- Do not open the door for anyone except law enforcement or verified IC.

## Nursery

- Lock suite immediately.
- Keep children calm; do not open for unknown persons until all clear.

## Pulpit / worship lead

- Stop service; one sentence: *"We are in lockdown — stay in your seats, teachers secure your rooms."*
- If Blue is playing, **do not** tell everyone to exit unless IC overrides.

## If the situation changes

Only IC (or backup) should switch codes. Often: **All clear** first, then **Code Red** if evacuation is ordered.
