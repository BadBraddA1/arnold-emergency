---
title: Communication checklist
description: What incident command should verify during an emergency.
---

During an incident, the **incident commander** should ensure:

- [ ] **911 called** (if required)
- [ ] **Code type** matches situation (Red vs. Blue)
- [ ] **Nursery / children** accounted for
- [ ] **Assembly or lockdown** areas communicated
- [ ] **One person** on official communication with first responders
- [ ] **All clear** authorized only by IC/backup
- [ ] **Post-incident debrief** scheduled within 72 hours

## External messaging

Congregation text, social media, press — only the designated spokesperson:

| Role | Name |
|------|------|
| **Spokesperson** | _[name]_ |

Do not post unverified details during an active incident.

## What the horns do vs. what people do

| Campus horns (Arnold Alert) | People |
|----------------------------|--------|
| Announce Code Red or Blue | Execute evacuation or lockdown |
| Loop until all clear | Call 911, sweep rooms, count kids |
| Play Code Green on all clear | Stand down per IC |

Horns do **not** call parents, lock doors, or tell rooms which exit to use — that is in this playbook and classroom handouts.
