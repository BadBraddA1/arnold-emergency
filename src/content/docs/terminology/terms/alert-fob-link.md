**Also called:** *Arm fob*, *Fob Arm*

## What it means

Links a campus fob to your staff PIN and arms it for **3 hours**. Unarmed fobs do nothing when pressed.

## How to use this term

App → **Arm fob**, or Alert Line 9090 → press **4** → PIN. Re-arm every 3 hours.
