title: System Standby
description: Horns stay silent

**Also called:** *Unarmed*

## What it means

Commands are **logged but held** — no audio on campus until a System Admin arms again.

## How to use this term

Maintenance, empty-building work, AV tests. Do not leave standby during public gatherings without reason.
