---
title: Fob Arm
description: 3-hour fob activation window
---


## What it means

The period when your assigned fob will actually trigger codes. Same 3-hour window as a normal app login session.

## How to use this term

Arm when you pick up a fob; re-arm if your shift is longer than 3 hours.
