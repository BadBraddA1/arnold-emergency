---
title: Incident commander
description: On-site authority during a crisis
---


**Also called:** *IC*

## What it means

The person who decides Red vs. Blue, ensures 911 is called, assigns nursery/class accountability, and **authorizes all clear**.

## How to use this term

Fill in names on the [Roles](/roles/) page. Backup IC assumes command if primary is unavailable.
