## What it means

The live source of truth for who does what during Code Red or Code Blue. Markdown in git, published at emergency.arnoldcoc.org.

## How to use this term

Say **Arnold Emergency** when you mean procedures and roles — not the horn system.

## Not the same as

- Arnold Alert (the audio/app system)
