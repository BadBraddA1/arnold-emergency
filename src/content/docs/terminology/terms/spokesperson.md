## What it means

The one person (plus backup) authorized to speak to press, post on official social media, and send congregation-wide messages during or after an incident. Works with the incident commander for facts but does not run the scene.

## How to use this term

Route all media and official social posts to the spokesperson. Follow the [media & social media playbook](/communication/#media-and-social-media-playbook).
