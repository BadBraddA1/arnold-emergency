## What it means

After you tap Code Red or Blue on a phone, a **10-second countdown** runs with a local phone alarm. Campus horns stay silent until you tap **Send now** or the timer hits zero.

## How to use this term

Lets you cancel a mistaken tap before the whole campus hears it.
