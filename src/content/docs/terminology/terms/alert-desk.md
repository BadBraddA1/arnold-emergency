**Also called:** *Desktop console*

## What it means

The management dashboard at alarm.arnoldcoc.org/desk/ — speakers, activity log, staff PINs, bells schedule, fobs, arm/standby.

## How to use this term

System Admins on a computer; not the first choice during an active crisis on your phone.
