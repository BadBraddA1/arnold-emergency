**Door cards** are the small text sheet posted **on or beside the door** — quick Red / Blue / 911 steps without the floor plan.

| Format | Where | Link |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Map poster** | On the wall (floor plan + route) | [Room evacuation posters](/classroom/room-posters/) |
| **Door card** | At the door (text only) | This page |

Find your room → **Print** → tape/laminate at the door. No app steps or IC jargon on either format.
